## Interpretation (carried into SG-04.3)

- `risk = -30` is the organisers' placeholder for Pc too small to compute. Kept as a value (Pc ≈ 0).
- `c_sigma_r` / `t_sigma_r` = 6.378136e7 is ten times Earth's radius in metres and repeats exactly, so it is treated as a suspected placeholder for unknown uncertainty: kept, flagged with an indicator feature, and excluded from the miss-distance/sigma ratio. Not confirmed with the organisers.
- `F10` = 66 and `AP` = 0 repeat at the minimum but are physically plausible (quiet solar and geomagnetic conditions), so they are kept as real values.
- `F10` and `AP` are missing together in about 4–5% of messages; imputed with an indicator.
- Most Critical labels come from miss distance, not Pc, so the model is effectively forecasting whether the final miss distance falls below 1 km.
