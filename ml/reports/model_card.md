# Model card

Generated by `python -m ml.model_card`. Decisions are in [docs/ML_DECISIONS.md](../../docs/ML_DECISIONS.md).

## What it does

Predicts each encounter's final tier (Safe / Elevated / Critical) from messages sent at least 2 days before closest approach. The organisers' tier rules are applied to the latest early message (persistence); a gradient-boosted classifier can then upgrade the tier to Critical when its score passes a threshold fixed on training folds. It never lowers a tier.

| Persistence (SG-05.1)               | 0.802 (0.730–0.865)        |                0.765 |             0.82  |          0.96 |               31 |                 2 |
| Upgrade, base (SG-05.2) **shipped** | 0.873 (0.817–0.929)        |                0.679 |             0.77  |          0.96 |               52 |                 2 |
| Upgrade, base_mre (SG-05.3)         | 0.833 (0.762–0.897)        |                0.734 |             0.799 |          0.96 |               38 |                 2 |

**Selected: upgrade, base.** It has the highest Critical recall. The cost is 21 more false Critical alarms than persistence, within the D7 budget of at most 3 false alarms per extra catch. `base_mre` adds `max_risk_estimate` but catches fewer Critical encounters, so the shipped model does not depend on it (D8). Confidence intervals overlap: the gain is real but modest. Upgrades also move some true Elevated encounters to Critical, so Elevated recall drops (0.82 → 0.77).

## Limitations

- Messages inside 2 days of closest approach are not used, so late geometry changes are invisible (see `failure_analysis.md`, e.g. `train:8732`).
- `p_critical` is a ranking score from class-weighted training, not a calibrated probability.
- Almost all Critical labels come from miss distance; only 3 validation encounters are Critical by Pc.
- The sigma value 6.378136e7 is treated as a placeholder; this is an inference, not confirmed.
- Validation only: there are no test outcomes, and no claim of operational reliability.
